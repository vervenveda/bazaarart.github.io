1 · Read and observe

Lesson A — What a line can do

A contour line traces a visible edge. A gesture line captures direction or movement. A light construction line helps position forms before the final drawing. Lines may vary in thickness, rhythm, and direction. Strong marks are not automatically more accurate.

Try it: Observe a cup. Draw its outline once. Then draw it again with a faint vertical axis, an ellipse for the rim, and side lines. Compare symmetry and proportions.

Lesson B — Geometric, organic, and empty shapes

Geometric shapes have regular properties; organic shapes are irregular. Artists can begin with circles, triangles, and rectangles, then refine the outline. Positive space contains the subject. Negative space surrounds it and appears between objects.

Try it: Draw an apple first as a circle, then with its observed silhouette. Draw the space between the apple and a nearby cup.

Lesson C — Proportion and revision

Proportion means the size relationships among parts. Composition means how elements are arranged. A recognizable object can still look unstable if its parts are misplaced. Compare widths and heights from one consistent viewing position. Revision should respond to what you actually observe.

Try it: Draw a mug from simple shapes, compare it with the real mug, and make a second version with one deliberate correction.

2 · Studio practice

Keep your drawings privately in a sketchbook or on your own device. This page does not upload images or request personal information.

Artist reflection

Younger learners may dictate their reflection to a trusted adult. Advanced learners can compare two compositions and their negative spaces.

3 · Check understanding

Ten questions are selected from a 16-question bank, with shuffled choices. The check provides explanatory feedback. A score of 80% or higher meets the knowledge-check threshold; studio mastery requires separate educator review.

4 · Studio mastery and evidence

Four educator-reviewed criteria: purposeful lines, shape analysis, proportion and negative space, and reflective revision. Each is scored 0–4. At least 13/16 meets the 80% standard. The learner's own checkbox marks and quiz score do not constitute educator approval or an official transcript.
