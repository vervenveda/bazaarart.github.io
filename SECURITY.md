# Security Policy

Bazaar Art welcomes responsible reports that help keep its public creative spaces safe, accessible, and trustworthy. Thank you for taking care with our community and its visitors.

This policy applies to security concerns involving the public Bazaar Art repository and experiences directly maintained by Bazaar Art. Connected destinations may be maintained separately and may have their own security reporting processes.

## Reporting a vulnerability

**Please do not publish an unpatched vulnerability, exploit instructions, credentials, or personal information in a public GitHub issue, pull request, discussion, or social post.**

1. Visit this repository's **Security** tab and use **Report a vulnerability** if private vulnerability reporting is available.
2. If that option is not available, use an official contact method published on the [Bazaar Art website](https://vervenveda.com/bazaarart.github.io/) to request a private reporting channel. Start with a brief, non-sensitive description only; wait for a secure channel before sharing technical details.
3. For non-sensitive security questions or documentation corrections, a public GitHub issue may be appropriate, but never include exploitable details or private data.

We do not publish a dedicated security email address in this file. Do not assume that a public issue, website form, or social account is confidential.

### Helpful information to include privately

- A concise description of the concern and its potential impact.
- The affected public page, feature, or repository file and the date observed.
- Reproduction steps using your own test data and accounts only.
- Browser, device, and relevant environment details.
- Redacted screenshots or a minimal, non-destructive proof of concept.
- Any suggested mitigation, if known.
- A way to follow up, if you are comfortable providing one.

Please redact tokens, cookies, passwords, private identifiers, unpublished artwork, and other people's information.

## What happens after a report

Reports will be reviewed and prioritized according to the evidence available and potential harm. We may ask clarifying questions, investigate the affected component, prepare a correction, and coordinate disclosure when appropriate.

**We cannot guarantee a specific response time, resolution deadline, reward, or individual reply.** A report is not confirmation that a vulnerability exists. Where a correction is made, public release notes or acknowledgments may be provided when appropriate and safe.

If you believe people face an immediate risk, avoid further testing or sharing sensitive material publicly; use the most direct official private reporting route available.

## Scope

Examples of concerns worth reporting include:

- Cross-site scripting, injection, or unsafe handling of visitor-supplied content.
- Unauthorized access to private information or another person's saved content.
- Exposed secrets, credentials, sensitive files, or unintended public data.
- Unsafe redirects, malicious links, or unexpected third-party script behavior.
- Authentication or authorization bypasses in an applicable Bazaar Art feature.
- Security-relevant dependency, browser storage, or content-loading flaws.
- Vulnerabilities that could materially affect visitor privacy, safety, or integrity.

A link from Bazaar Art to another website does not mean Bazaar Art operates or can fix that website. Report issues in third-party services to their respective maintainers. If a linked destination creates a safety risk on Bazaar Art, you may also report the link to us.

## Responsible research guidelines

We appreciate good-faith research conducted with restraint. Please:

- Use only accounts, devices, content, and data that you own or are expressly authorized to test.
- Keep testing minimal, non-destructive, and proportionate to confirming a concern.
- Stop if you encounter another person's private information; do not retain, copy, or distribute it.
- Do not attempt social engineering, phishing, credential attacks, denial of service, traffic flooding, or service disruption.
- Do not modify or delete other people's data, bypass payment processes, or access restricted systems.
- Do not upload malicious files or run persistence, malware, or broad automated scans.
- Give maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate before public disclosure.

This policy does **not** grant permission to access systems or data without authorization, waive applicable laws, or promise legal safe harbor.

## Privacy and community protection

Bazaar Art aims to respect visitor privacy and creative ownership. Do not submit personal data, confidential material, or third-party artwork in a vulnerability report unless strictly necessary and expressly authorized. Use fictional or redacted examples whenever possible.

If you discover information that appears inadvertently exposed, report its location and general nature privately. Avoid opening additional records, indexing the material, or sharing copies.

## Supported versions and updates

The current public version of this repository is the primary focus for security review. Historical commits, archived copies, forks, and independently hosted mirrors may not receive fixes. Security-related improvements may be applied as needed; this file is not a promise of a fixed release schedule.

## Security versus general feedback

For ordinary broken links, visual issues, accessibility suggestions, feature requests, or non-sensitive bugs, use the repository's regular feedback channels. Use private reporting when disclosure could enable exploitation or reveal private information.

## Coordinated disclosure and credit

Please coordinate public disclosure with maintainers so that users can be protected first. If you would like acknowledgment, say so in your private report; attribution is considered case by case and will not include personal information without permission.

## Policy changes

This policy may be updated as Bazaar Art evolves. Check the latest version in this repository for current guidance.
