Materials, Surface and Texture

Weeks 20–22 · Material investigation, collage and relief

Unit inquiry

How do material properties, physical surfaces and deliberate marks shape the meaning of original art? Learners will compare observed and implied texture, construct mixed-media works and document revisions with cited research.

Art 101 is proficiency-based for teens and adults. Practice is not authenticated mastery; evidence remains browser-local until an authorized educator reviews it.
