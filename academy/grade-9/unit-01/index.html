Unit 01 · Seeing and Visual Inquiry

Four original investigations in observation, expressive line, negative space, and visual interpretation. These studio exercises are formative; the quiz is a practice check, not an Academy mastery certification.

Unit 01 · Start with the artist's portfolio

Open Week 01 · My Artist Portfolio to complete the foundational assignment: create an artwork archive, label your studies, investigate an artist's process, document an original observational drawing, and revise it using evidence.

The interactive investigations below are an additional formative drawing studio. They do not replace the dedicated weekly lessons or certify Academy mastery. After completing your portfolio, continue to Week 02 · Contour and Careful Seeing.

Week 03 · Visual Composition Laboratory

Move shapes to investigate occupied and empty space, balance, overlap, and focal emphasis. The laboratory is available at any time; it is especially relevant to Week 03. Use pointer drag or select a shape and use the arrow buttons for keyboard-accessible positioning.

Study question: How do the areas between shapes change when one form moves? Record your observations in the evidence journal. The approximate occupied-area indicator counts overlapping pixels once and is an exploratory measure, not a grade.

Practice knowledge check

Ten randomized questions from a dedicated 12-question bank for each week (48 questions across this unit). At least 80% is the practice target. Attempts are regenerated and submitted answers cannot be changed within the graded attempt.

Authentic studio review rubric

A reviewer scores actual artwork and academic evidence; practice quizzes do not replace studio assessment. This local worksheet is not a certified Academy grade.

Tool and game integration

We will connect existing Academy and Arcade tools only after confirming their exact live destinations and classroom suitability. This original drawing laboratory is the first built-in creative tool; its output can be saved independently as PNG.

Local notes are device/browser-specific. No learner authentication, portfolio submission, social sharing, or STOS terminal connection is enabled here.
