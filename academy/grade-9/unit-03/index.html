KHAEMENES ACADEMY · GRADE 9 · UNIT 03

Unit 03 · Color and Visual Meaning

Weeks 05–07 · Three independent studio assignments

From observing form to making color decisions

Unit 02 developed contour, proportion, and value. Unit 03 examines how artists organize hue, value, saturation, and contrast to communicate meaning. Every week produces a dated artwork, process evidence, and a written reflection for the portfolio established in Week 01.

One original assignment per week. Formative checks support learning; a teacher or guardian reviews final studio evidence. Browser-local drafts are not authenticated submissions.

Weekly studios

Week 05 · Hue, Value and Saturation

Compare color dimensions, investigate accessible color contrast, and make an original three-panel color study.

Open Week 05 lesson

Week 06 · Color Relationships

Analogous, complementary and split-complementary palettes, a color-blending laboratory, and an original composition.

Open Week 06 lesson

Week 07 · Color, Place and Meaning

Investigate lighting, material, place and documented color interpretation through an interactive visual laboratory and original artwork.

Open Week 07 lesson

Portfolio connection

Use your existing portfolio catalog. Save the Week 05 swatches, a photograph or scan of your original artwork, and an artist statement explaining each color choice.
