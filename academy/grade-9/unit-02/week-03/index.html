KHAEMENES ACADEMY · GRADE 9 · UNIT 02

Week 3 · Proportion and Relationships

Relative measurement · Negative space · Revision

Essential question

How can artists estimate proportion without guessing?

Proportion compares the sizes of parts within a whole. Relative measurement uses one observed dimension as a reference. Negative space is the visible space around and between forms; comparing its shapes can reveal errors in a drawing.

Observe

Arrange a mug, book, and small object. Choose one dimension as your unit and compare other heights and widths to it.

Measure

Hold a pencil at arm’s length, close one eye, and mark a reference length with your thumb. Keep your arm at the same distance.

Revise

Check at least two negative spaces between objects. Redraw one incorrect relationship without tracing the original.

Worked example · Comparing height and width

For this illustrative object, the height-to-width ratio is 4:3. If your drawing is 12 cm high, a matching body width is 9 cm. Real objects vary: measure your actual subject rather than copying these example numbers.

One weekly assignment · Proportion study

  1. Make a thumbnail of a three-object still life.
  2. Record at least four relative measurements in a small table (reference, observed ratio, drawn ratio, correction).
  3. Draw the positive shapes and at least two negative-space shapes.
  4. Complete a final observational drawing with revised proportions.
  5. Archive the first attempt, final drawing, and written comparison as one Week 03 assignment.

Materials: pencil, eraser, paper, ruler or straight edge, and three safe household objects. No camera or paid materials required.

Interactive proportion laboratory

Change the example drawing height and width. Compare its ratio to the observed reference ratio of 4:3. This models measurement reasoning; it does not measure a real object for you.

Reasoning check · Practice, not certification

Academic evidence · Week 3

Preserve your studies and final drawing in your physical portfolio. This local record supports reflection but does not submit work or award an Academy grade.

Reviewer rubric · 100 points

Observation and proportional reasoning (25); evidence of measurement (20); negative-space study (20); revision and accuracy (15); written analysis (15); archival labels (5).

The proposed mastery threshold is 80%, subject to an authorized reviewer. The practice check and browser-local evidence do not establish certified mastery.
