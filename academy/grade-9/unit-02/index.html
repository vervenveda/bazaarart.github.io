Unit 02 · Seeing and Drawing

Weeks 02–04 · Three original weekly assignments · One assignment per page

From keeping art to making art

Unit 01 established the artist's portfolio. Unit 02 begins close observation: noticing contours, measuring relationships, and building value through light and shadow. Each week contributes a labeled artwork, a process study, and a reflection to the same portfolio.

The 36-week course retains 13 units. Unit 01 is the complete portfolio-building unit; Unit 02 covers three weeks. Subsequent unit durations will be mapped explicitly rather than assuming every unit is equal.

Three weekly studio assignments

Week 02 · Contour and careful seeing

Observe an everyday object, draw continuous and modified contours, and explain how observation changed your marks.

Enter Week 02 studio

Week 03 · Proportion and relationships

Measure relative sizes and negative spaces in a small still life. Individual lesson page forthcoming.

Week 04 · Value and form

Investigate a five-step value scale and apply it to a simple object. Individual lesson page forthcoming.

Portfolio continuity

Keep your Week 01 cover and catalog. Date each drawing, note the medium and technique, and preserve at least one early attempt alongside the final work. Browser-local records are not an authenticated Academy submission.
