KHAEMENES ACADEMY · ART 101 · UNIT 04

Unit 04 · Composition and Visual Storytelling

Weeks 08–10 · Three original weekly artworks

Organizing what the viewer sees

After studying contour, proportion, value, and color, artists learn to arrange these elements intentionally. Unit 04 investigates focal points, balance, movement, scale, and visual narrative. Each week produces one original artwork with planning studies and an evidence record.

Art 101 is proficiency-based and welcomes teen and adult learners. The legacy grade-9 folder paths and evidence keys remain for compatibility. Each lesson includes randomized formative practice; reaching 80% in practice is not authenticated mastery or educator approval.

Weekly studios

Week 08 · Focal Point and Visual Hierarchy

Investigate placement, scale, value contrast, and directional cues using an interactive composition laboratory and create one original focal-point artwork.
