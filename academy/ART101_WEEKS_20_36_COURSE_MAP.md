# Art 101 — Weeks 20–36 instructional continuation

Status: authored course map; lessons and assessments for Weeks 20–36 are **not yet implemented**. Keep existing Weeks 01–19 and their legacy evidence keys intact. Art 101 is proficiency-based; legacy grade-9 paths are compatibility routes, not age restrictions.

## Unit 08 — Materials, Surface and Texture (Weeks 20–22)
- **Week 20 — Material Marks and Texture.** Compare graphite, ink, charcoal and found mark-making tools; produce a controlled texture atlas, three media tests, and a researched visual analysis.
- **Week 21 — Collage, Layering and Material Meaning.** Investigate transparent/opaque layers, edges, adhesion, sourcing and attribution; make an original mixed-media composition with process photographs or written observations.
- **Week 22 — Surface, Relief and Revision.** Explore tactile versus implied texture, low-relief construction and lighting; create a safe relief study, critique it and document revisions.

## Unit 09 — Printmaking and Reproducible Images (Weeks 23–25)
- **Week 23 — Positive and Negative Shape.** Design a two-color print matrix; compare figure/ground and mirror reversal using safe paper or foam impressions.
- **Week 24 — Registration, Edition and Variation.** Test alignment, pressure, repeated impressions and intentional variation; archive a small numbered edition and failure analysis.
- **Week 25 — Print as Public Communication.** Research historic and contemporary print traditions with attribution; create a message-based print series and an audience-impact reflection.

## Unit 10 — Photography and Visual Evidence (Weeks 26–28)
- **Week 26 — Framing, Light and Camera Position.** Make a contact-sheet-style observational study using a camera if available, or viewfinder drawings if not; document framing decisions.
- **Week 27 — Sequence, Documentary Ethics and Context.** Analyze caption accuracy, consent, staging and context; create a responsible six-frame visual sequence with source notes.
- **Week 28 — Photo Essay and Editorial Revision.** Develop a cohesive visual essay with captions, sequencing, critique and a clear distinction between observation and interpretation; allow a fully drawn alternative.

## Unit 11 — Typography, Graphic Design and Information (Weeks 29–31)
- **Week 29 — Letterform and Readability.** Investigate hierarchy, spacing, contrast and accessibility; produce original lettering studies and a legibility test.
- **Week 30 — Layout, Grids and Visual Systems.** Create modular layouts, compare audience navigation and revise a one-page informational composition.
- **Week 31 — Information Design and Ethical Claims.** Design an evidence-grounded infographic; label sources, scale and uncertainty; conduct a peer or educator comprehension review.

## Unit 12 — Art, Community and Curatorial Practice (Weeks 32–34)
- **Week 32 — Art in Cultural Context.** Research artworks with careful attribution and respectful contextualization; produce a comparative visual analysis without reducing cultures to stereotypes.
- **Week 33 — Community Brief and Collaborative Design.** Write an audience-aware creative brief, solicit optional feedback and build a revised proposal with privacy-aware documentation.
- **Week 34 — Curating an Exhibition.** Select, sequence and label works; draft wall texts, access considerations and a floor plan for a physical or paper-based exhibition.

## Unit 13 — Capstone, Defense and Portfolio (Weeks 35–36)
- **Week 35 — Independent Capstone Studio.** Propose, research, prototype and execute an original work synthesizing at least three prior course techniques; record iterations and limitations.
- **Week 36 — Portfolio Exhibition and Artist Defense.** Curate representative work, cite influences, present a written or oral defense, document revision and complete educator-reviewed final evidence.
