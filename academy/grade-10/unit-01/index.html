Unit 01 · The Evolving Artist

Evidence of growth · Artistic intention · Revision · Portfolio continuity

From foundation to intentional practice

Grade 9 established the artist's first record of observation, experiments, and completed work. Grade 10 begins by asking how that evidence can guide increasingly independent artistic decisions. New learners can create baseline studies now; no earlier Academy enrollment is required.

Open Week 01 · The Evolving Artist

Seven standards guiding this unit

Academic depth

Distinguish observation, inference, intention, and evidence.

Demonstration

Study a comparable before-and-after drawing investigation.

Physical studio

Produce original studies and a selected finished work.

Experimentation

Test three purposeful compositional alternatives.

Critical thinking

Research documented artist processes and cite sources.

Assessment and revision

Apply descriptors, critique choices, and document changes.

Portfolio evidence

Preserve labeled drafts, final artwork, reflection, and goals.

Unit 01 studio outcome

Complete a growth-oriented portfolio with a documented baseline, three compositional alternatives, a selected artwork, one critique-informed revision, and an artist statement. The existing Week 01 lesson provides the interactive cover designer, artwork catalog, printable labels, local evidence tools, and rubric.

Review by an authorized Academy teacher or guardian is separate from this page; the unit overview does not issue mastery certification.
