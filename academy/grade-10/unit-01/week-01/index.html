Week 01 · The Evolving Artist

Unit 01 · Portfolio Growth and Technical Intent · One original studio assignment

Essential question

How can evidence from earlier work guide deliberate artistic improvement?

Learning objectives

Compare earlier and current techniques; identify a specific strength and growth area; design an organized second-year portfolio; establish measurable technical goals.

A portfolio is a living archive of process and finished work. Students may maintain a physical, offline digital, or combined portfolio. No social account or public posting is required.

Original lesson · Study, plan, and create

Read your artistic evidence

Select two earlier artworks, or two initial studies if you are new to the program. Distinguish observable features from personal judgment. Identify differences in control, composition, experimentation, and documentation.

Organize for comparison

Create portfolio divisions for baseline work, skill investigations, completed pieces, critique, and revisions. Preserve dates and attribution so that changes can be traced.

Design a growth-oriented cover

Create an original cover or title spread that uses a visual motif to communicate change, continuity, or new direction. Prepare two thumbnails before committing to the final design.

Worked portfolio framework

Document each stage with a date, an artifact or observation, and a short explanation. The sequence may loop: critique can lead to new inquiry.

Week 01 · One studio assignment

  1. Choose two prior works or create two baseline studies using the same subject.
  2. Record three evidence-based observations about each work.
  3. Write two specific technical goals and how future works will demonstrate progress.
  4. Design and complete an original portfolio cover or opening spread.
  5. File the baseline evidence, cover thumbnails, final design, and reflection.

Submission: One completed portfolio cover or opening spread, process studies, organized evidence, and a reflection. Use affordable or reused materials where possible. The portfolio remains private unless an authorized learner or guardian chooses otherwise.
