Unit 01 · The Artist's Legacy

Curatorial thesis · Original artwork · Accessible exhibition · Artistic reflection

Every senior artist has a place to begin

Whether you are entering Khaemenes Academy for the first time or continuing from an earlier grade, your Grade 12 portfolio begins with the same essential tools: a cover designer, artwork catalog, printable labels, local evidence and a 36-week growth timeline. Returning students may select earlier work; new students create their own baseline collection. Public posting is not required.

Open Week 01 · The Artist's Legacy

Seven standards guiding this unit

Academic depth

Explain a curatorial thesis and defend artistic selection criteria.

Demonstration

Study a worked exhibition sequence and interpretive label.

Physical studio

Curate three to five original works in physical, printed or private digital form.

Experimentation

Compare alternative exhibition arrangements and visual rhythms.

Critical thinking

Research documented exhibitions, cultural context and ethical presentation.

Assessment and revision

Evaluate the thesis, labels, accessibility and audience experience.

Portfolio evidence

Preserve original art, curatorial notes, revisions, sources and artist statement.

Senior creative progression · Exhibition and portfolio book

Grade 12 develops a professional presentation: a curated sequence, interpretive labels, artist biography, and exhibition statement. A dedicated interactive exhibition and portfolio-book builder is planned, but is not yet active. The existing portfolio tools remain available now.

Unit 01 outcome

Produce a miniature exhibition with three to five original works, a supported curatorial thesis, documented research, accessible labels, a 250–400 word artist statement, and evidence of one revision. Formal mastery decisions remain with the authorized Academy workflow.
