Week 01 · The Artist's Legacy

Unit 01 · Professional Portfolio and Exhibition Planning · One original studio assignment

Essential question

How can a curated body of work communicate an artist's sustained inquiry and growth?

Learning objectives

Define a curatorial thesis; select and sequence works using criteria; prepare professional labels and an artist statement; plan an accessible exhibition presentation.

A portfolio is a living archive of process and finished work. Students may maintain a physical, offline digital, or combined portfolio. No social account or public posting is required.

Original lesson · Study, plan, and create

From archive to curated collection

A process portfolio preserves the journey. A presentation portfolio selects works for an audience. Neither is simply a popularity ranking; selection should communicate intention, technical range, revision, and inquiry.

Design the viewing experience

Consider order, visual rhythm, label clarity, accessibility, and whether the exhibition is physical, private digital, or printed. Avoid public sharing by default.

Write as an artist

Draft a concise statement identifying your recurring question, materials, choices, and how your work evolved. Make claims grounded in actual portfolio evidence.

Worked portfolio framework

Document each stage with a date, an artifact or observation, and a short explanation. The sequence may loop: critique can lead to new inquiry.

Week 01 · One studio assignment

  1. Inventory at least five existing artworks, or create a documented selection plan if fewer are available.
  2. Choose a curatorial theme and explain selection criteria for three candidate works.
  3. Make two exhibition sequence thumbnails and justify the final order.
  4. Design a professional portfolio cover and create labels for the selected works.
  5. Draft an artist statement and a short accessibility and presentation plan.

Submission: One completed portfolio cover or opening spread, process studies, organized evidence, and a reflection. Use affordable or reused materials where possible. The portfolio remains private unless an authorized learner or guardian chooses otherwise.
