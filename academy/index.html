Learning across a lifetime

Four developmental pathways

Pre-K–K

Discover

Color, texture, line, shapes, sensory investigation, and imaginative play. Guided making with caregiver participation.

Grades 1–5

Observe

Visual storytelling, nature journals, color relationships, composition, craft, and respectful cultural inquiry.

Grades 6–8

Investigate

Perspective, value, design principles, printmaking, art history, sculpture, critique, and process portfolios.

Grades 9–12 & adults

Develop a voice

Rigorous studio practice, research, medium specialization, artist statements, portfolio building, and exhibition.
