# Grade 9 · Foundations of Visual Art · Canonical 36-Week Map

Status: canonical Bazaar Art Academy source curriculum for Khaemenes High School integration.

## Canonical sequence
- Unit 01 · Week 01 · Artist portfolio, baseline and archive
- Unit 02 · Weeks 02–04 · Seeing, contour, proportion and form
- Unit 03 · Weeks 05–07 · Color systems and meaning
- Unit 04 · Weeks 08–10 · Composition, hierarchy and visual narrative
- Unit 05 · Weeks 11–13 · Observed form, light and figure
- Unit 06 · Weeks 14–16 · Perspective, viewpoint and spatial narrative
- Unit 07 · Weeks 17–19 · Composition, rhythm and visual communication
- Unit 08 · Weeks 20–22 · Materials, surface and texture
- Unit 09 · Weeks 23–25 · Printmaking and reproducible images
- Unit 10 · Weeks 26–28 · Photography and visual evidence
- Unit 11 · Weeks 29–31 · Typography, graphic design and information
- Unit 12 · Weeks 32–34 · Art, community and curatorial practice
- Unit 13 · Weeks 35–36 · Capstone, portfolio and Artist Defense

Course architecture: 36 weeks, 13 units, weekly authentic studio evidence, educator-facing 100-point rubrics, randomized formative practice, no purchase requirement, no public-posting requirement, portfolio evidence, attribution, critique and revision throughout.
