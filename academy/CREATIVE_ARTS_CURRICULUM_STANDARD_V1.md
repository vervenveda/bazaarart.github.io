# Bazaar Art × Khaemenes Academy — Creative Arts Curriculum Standard v1

Status: instructional design specification; **not** an accredited program, certified credit equivalency, or deployed Academy mastery integration.

## Mission
Teach art as sustained inquiry, skilled making, critical visual literacy, cultural study, and original expression. Students observe, hypothesize, practice, create, critique, revise, document, and present. Art games and tools are formative laboratories, never substitutes for authentic artwork or independent judgment.

## Required weekly anatomy (grades 6–12)
1. Essential question, measurable artistic/critical objectives, and prerequisites.
2. Original reading with historical and cultural context; distinguish documented facts, contested interpretations, and personal responses.
3. Visual worked demonstration with accessible text alternative and step-by-step technique.
4. At least one original observational/technical investigation, with accessible non-digital option.
5. Three studio activities: exploration, independent original production, and critique/revision.
6. Vocabulary with definitions and contextual use.
7. One or more relevant optional games/tools, including their learning purpose and limitations.
8. Formative misconception checks and a sufficiently large reviewed question bank; record bank size and coverage by learning objective.
9. An authentic studio artifact plus Academic Evidence Convention fields.
10. Reflection, reviewer guidance, and a clear next-week bridge.

## Assessment design
- Weekly formative checks: 10 items selected from a reviewed, objective-aligned pool of at least 30 items, with question and option randomization, locked graded attempts, and meaningful per-item feedback. Do not certify mastery based on games alone.
- Weekly authentic studio rubric (suggested 100 points): Observation and investigation 20; technical application 20; original creative decisions 20; evidence and attribution 15; critique/revision 15; communication/presentation 10. A score of at least 80/100 is the proposed studio mastery threshold, subject to Academy approval and authentic reviewer authority.
- Midterm and final: independent studio tasks and critical visual analysis with closed-book portions where appropriate, plus reviewer authentication. Proposed 80% thresholds.
- Grade 9–12 graduation from each elective requires all 36 weekly gates, midterm, final, and a coherent reviewed portfolio. **No automatic credit award** until Academy authority, identity, persistence, and permission systems are integrated and certified.
- Pre-K–Grade 5 use developmentally appropriate observation and human review; never use automated high-stakes visual judgments on children's artwork.

## Academic Evidence Convention
Each printable/exportable packet identifies Academy, learner (optional until authenticated), date, grade, course, unit, week, lesson; research question; cited sources; vocabulary; studies and materials; observations/calculations/writing; claims and limitations; interdisciplinary connections; parent/teacher review; artwork references and revision history. Public browser storage is not authenticated identity or secure cross-device synchronization.

## Grade 9 Unit 01 — distinct week specifications
- Week 01: Contour observation. Students distinguish observed edges from invented symbols; produce two contours with annotated corrections. Tool: Contour Drawing Laboratory.
- Week 02: Gesture and expressive line. Students test duration, directional movement, and line weight; produce three gesture studies and a revised expressive composition. Tool: Gesture Studio.
- Week 03: Positive/negative space. Students analyze occupied and empty shapes, design two contrasting arrangements, and explain balance and visual weight. Tool: Composition Explorer.
- Week 04: Visual inquiry and critique. Students separate description, analysis, interpretation, and evaluation; cite an artwork, defend two evidence-based claims, and produce an original response study. Tool: Art Detective.

## Tool integration contract
Every tool must have a verified route, a declared instructional objective, accessible keyboard/touch alternative where relevant, privacy boundaries, and an offline or paper fallback. Existing Bazaar Art, Arcade, Academy, and ProReSources tools are candidates for inspection, **not yet verified integrations**. New original labs should be standalone vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, without external script dependencies or student-data transmission.

## Safety and culture
- No public posting, social-account coupling, or private evidence transfer without verified authorization and guardian controls where applicable.
- Teach material/tool safety and low-cost alternatives; avoid implying all families can buy specialist equipment.
- Attribute artists and collections, respect copyrights and cultural context, avoid treating one culture as representative of all.
- Preserve learner choice, accessibility, non-visual alternatives, and respectful critique.
- STOS terminal connectivity remains a separate future infrastructure project.

## Certification gates before Academy profile integration
1. Curriculum completeness audit (all lessons and original assets).
2. Source and art-history fact check.
3. Question bank review, objective coverage, randomized-attempt integrity.
4. Browser regressions: fresh attempt, submit, lock, retake, reload, export, print, offline, mobile and keyboard.
5. Fictional two-learner isolation, identity authorization, guardian/reviewer boundaries.
6. Verified Academy grade/credit rules and 80% gating.
7. Link and tool registry validation; no broken or placeholder buttons.
8. Explicit release approval after evidence is recorded.
