Sources help us discover opportunities, but the registry still distinguishes a discovery listing from verification on the official organizer page.

Community Experience Reviews

Artist remarks are shown as lived experience, not as proof that a call is valid or invalid. Public remarks can be moderated by the repository owner; local remarks remain in the artist’s own browser and exports.

Validity ≠ Popularity

Verification checks official facts. Reviews describe artist experience. They are intentionally separate.

Respectful, Specific Remarks

Describe what happened: communication, payment, exhibition handling, deadlines, contracts and follow-through. Avoid doxxing or unsupported accusations.

The registry itself is always free and ad-free. Individual organizations may charge application fees; those fees are disclosed and filterable. Always confirm the organizer’s official terms before applying.
